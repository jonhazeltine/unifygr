# What the published calendars actually contain

Searched every event The Church Map publishes for Grand Rapids (765 over 60 days)
for the categories that have no theme of their own, so nobody has to repeat it.

Run again with `scripts/convert-events-to-offerings.py` after a calendar rebuild.

## Nothing published, anywhere in the network

singles · marriage prep and enrichment · divorce recovery · recovery and
addiction · ESL and literacy · special needs and disability · homeschool ·
MOPS and mothers of preschoolers · job and employment · veterans and first
responders · refugee and immigrant · prison and reentry

These are not empty because we stopped looking. Grand Rapids churches do not put
them on a calendar. For most of them the answer is a specialist rather than a
church, which is why they sit on the specialised list.

## Published, but not carriable

- **Grief** — 10 events, two sources. The Roman Catholic Diocese runs GriefShare
  weekly; First Presbyterian runs a group in Holland, 29.7 miles out. One is a
  tradition we stay away from, the other is beyond our radius, so the category
  is carried by a last-resort listing instead.
- **Foster care** — one Mass and picnic, Roman Catholic Diocese.
- **College** — nothing. The only keyword hit is "Jolly Seniors (off campus)".

## What this means for curation

The gap in these categories closes by conversation, not by searching. Either a
church starts publishing a calendar we can read, or a person here confirms dates
by hand through the watcher (`scripts/watch-announcements.py`).
